About The Festival

THE RENAISSANCE 2026 is a two-day multidisciplinary festival bringing together science, innovation, entrepreneurship, technology, literature, art, creativity, gaming, and culture under one platform.

Our Vision

To establish an inclusive platform where knowledge, creativity, and innovation converge to inspire the next generation.

The Five Festival Wings

30 distinctive competitions and exhibitions across 5 specialized wings

I. THE GENESIS — Innovation & Entrepreneurship

1. Project Exhibition

Showcase scientific, technological, and creative projects demonstrating practical applications.

2. Start-up Pitch

Aspiring entrepreneurs present business ideas and demonstrate venture potential.

3. Young Innovators

A showcase of original ideas, inventions, and innovative solutions by young creators.

II. THE CHRONICLE — Art, Creativity & Visual Expression

1. Wall Magazine - Senior

Creative publication featuring ideas, illustrations, and informative content.

2. Wall Magazine - Junior

Collaborative creative publications for junior participants.

3. Art Exhibition

A showcase of original artworks and artistic expressions.

4. Junior Artist Competition

A creative competition encouraging young artists.

5. Scrapbook Display

Presentation of creatively arranged photographs, memories, and visual narratives.

6. Open Themed Illustration

Drawing and illustration competition based on a selected theme (Pre-submission).

7. Photography Competition

Visual storytelling through photography (Pre-submission).

III. THE EXPRESSION — Literature, Communication & Debate

1. Extempore Speech

Spontaneous public speaking on assigned topics.

2. Creative Writing

A literary competition encouraging original storytelling.

3. Asian Parliamentary Debate

Structured debate competition focusing on argumentation, reasoning, and rebuttal.

4. Original Poetry Writing

Participants present original poems expressing their thoughts and imagination.

IV. THE ATHENAEUM — Science, Knowledge & Intelligence

1. STEM Olympiad - Senior

Science, technology, engineering, and mathematics competition for seniors.

2. STEM Olympiad - Junior

STEM knowledge competition for junior participants.

3. Quiz Competition - Senior

Competitive assessment of academic and general knowledge.

4. Quiz Competition - Junior

Knowledge competition designed for junior participants.

5. Mega Team Quiz

Team-based quiz featuring preliminary, semi-final, and final rounds.

6. Marvel vs DC Quiz

Themed knowledge competition based on Marvel and DC universes.

7. Pottermore Quiz

Themed knowledge competition inspired by the Wizarding World.

8. IQ Test

A challenge focusing on logical reasoning and analytical thinking.

9. Old College Quiz

Knowledge competition based on educational, historical, and general topics.

V. THE ODYSSEY — Technology, Robotics, Gaming & Strategy

1. Free Fire

Competitive mobile gaming event testing teamwork and strategy.

2. EA SPORTS FC 26

Football gaming competition featuring competitive matches.

3. E-Football

Digital football competition focused on gaming skills.

4. Line Following Robot (LFR)

Robotics competition where robots follow a predefined track.

5. Mini Sumo

Robotics competition featuring small robots in a designated arena.

6. Chess Showdown

A strategic board-game competition.

7. Treasure Hunt

Interactive challenge involving clues, problem-solving, and exploration.
